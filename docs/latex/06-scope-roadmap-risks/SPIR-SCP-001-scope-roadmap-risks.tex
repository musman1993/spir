\section{Purpose}
This document sets expectations. It states the scope of work delivered in release \SPIRRelease, what is deliberately
left out, the limitations a user or vendor will meet, the risks with their owners and treatment, and a phased
roadmap to production. Detailed per-requirement status is in SPIR-RTM-001.

\section{Limitations}
These are the limits a user or a vendor will meet in release \SPIRRelease.

\begin{caution}[Not ready for real data on a public address]
The sign-in is \code{admin} / \code{admin} for both portals and is the same for everyone. QR scans are open by
design. Use real data only on a private address (for example a Codespace with private ports) until Phase 1 is done.
\end{caution}

\subsection{Access and accounts}
\begin{itemize}
  \item One shared supplier account that belongs to one supplier at a time. Several suppliers cannot work in parallel
    without changing the profile.
  \item One shared company account; actions are audited as ``Company admin'', not per person.
  \item No password change, lock-out or session revocation screen; sessions expire after 12 hours.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Data and workflow}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Saves are live at once: there is no review before data reaches a QR scan. Mistakes are corrected by a new
    version; the wrong version stays in the history.
  \item Part number, PO and line cannot be changed after saving; a wrong one means a new shipment and revoking the
    old label.
  \item No deletion of shipments (by design for traceability); no supplier deactivation screen.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{QR labels and scanning}
\begin{itemize}
  \item QR codes contain the web address set in \code{PUBLIC\_APP\_URL}. Labels printed with a Codespace address need a
    GitHub sign-in on the phone and stop working when the Codespace is deleted.
  \item Anyone holding a label (or a photo of it) can see that shipment and its certificates.
  \item The rate limiter lives in each function instance; it is a speed bump, not a guarantee.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Label check (OCR)}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Tesseract.js reads full-frame phone photos poorly (26--35\,\% word confidence in our tests). Good results need a
    tight crop of the label and original-resolution photos; glare and stamps can make a label unresolvable.
  \item Only the \code{RECEIVING\_LABELS v1} profile exists; new label layouts need a profile change.
  \item No automatic deskew or perspective correction.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Operations}
\begin{itemize}
  \item No metrics, alerting, backups policy or tested restore; Neon's built-in point-in-time restore is not configured
    or tested.
  \item The Neon production database had only migration 001 applied at the time of writing.
  \item Credentials pasted during set-up (database password, storage keys, QR key) must be treated as exposed and
    rotated (see risk R-04).
\end{itemize}

\section{Risk register}
Likelihood (L) and impact (I): High, Medium, Low.

{\small\zebra
\begin{xltabular}{\linewidth}{@{}L{1.0cm}Y L{1.3cm}L{1.3cm}Y L{1.8cm}@{}}
  \thead\hd{ID} & \hd{Risk} & \hd{L} & \hd{I} & \hd{Treatment} & \hd{Owner}\\\endhead
  R-01 & Shared admin/admin credentials allow anyone who learns them to change data. & \High & \High & Keep on private address; Phase 1 RBAC and SSO before go-live. & Business owner\\
  R-02 & Public QR scans expose shipment data and certificates to anyone with the label. & \Med & \Med & Decision recorded (ADR-09); confirm or require company sign-in before production; revoke leaked labels. & Business owner\\
  R-03 & No review before data is live: a supplier error reaches receiving immediately. & \Med & \Med & Label check catches mismatches; Phase 1 restores approval workflow. & Quality\\
  R-04 & Credentials were shared in chat during set-up. & \High & \High & Rotate the Neon database password, storage keys and QR key; never paste \code{.env}. & IT / author\\
  R-05 & OCR accuracy on real photos is not measured on a representative set. & \Med & \Med & Collect 100+ labelled photos; measure precision/recall per field (NFR-OCR-001). & Quality\\
  R-06 & QR labels printed with a temporary address stop working. & \Med & \High & Set \code{PUBLIC\_APP\_URL} to the permanent address before printing for real parts. & IT\\
  R-07 & Losing \code{QR\_TOKEN\_KEY} prevents reprinting existing labels (scans still work). & \Low & \Med & Store the key in a password manager and GitHub secret. & IT\\
  R-08 & Free-tier hosting limits (Neon, Codespaces) interrupt demos. & \Med & \Low & Move to paid tier or company hosting in Phase 2. & IT\\
  R-09 & Uploaded files are not malware-scanned. & \Low & \High & Add scanning in Phase 2; keep the bucket private. & IT\\
  R-10 & Real data committed to the public repository by mistake. & \Low & \High & Real data only in git-ignored \code{seed/local/}; review pull requests. & Developers\\
\end{xltabular}}

\begin{keypoint}[Decisions needed from the business owner]
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Keep QR scans public, or require a company sign-in (affects \req{FR-QR-003}, \req{FR-DOC-005})?
  \item When must the approval step return, and who reviews?
  \item Which permanent web address will be printed in QR codes?
  \item Who owns the risks R-01, R-02 and R-04, and the Phase 1 budget?
\end{enumerate}
\end{keypoint}
