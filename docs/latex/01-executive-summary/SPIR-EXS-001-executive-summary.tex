\section{The problem}
Receiving inspection checks every incoming part against its purchase order, heat number, quantity and certificates.
Today that information arrives on paper labels, e-mails and PDFs, and is matched by eye. Wrong heats, wrong quantities
and missing certificates are found late, and there is no single record of what the supplier declared.

\section{What SPIR does}
SPIR is a company-owned registry with \textbf{one record per shipment} and \textbf{one QR label per shipment}:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Suppliers} enter shipment data in their portal (form or CSV), attach certificates, and print QR labels
    for their parts.
  \item \textbf{At receiving}, anyone scans the label with a phone to see the shipment and open its certificates.
  \item \textbf{Company staff} search all shipments and check the supplier's own sticker against the registry from a
    photo: SPIR reads it (OCR and barcodes), finds the shipment and compares every field.
  \item \textbf{Every change is kept}: history cannot be overwritten and every action is audited.
\end{itemize}

\begin{keypoint}[In short]
A working proof of concept covering supplier data entry, QR labels that phone cameras read reliably, public scanning,
company search and an OCR label check, on the agreed stack (GitHub, Neon). Of the \rtm{all}{all} requirements in
spec v1.1, \rtm{all}{I} are implemented and \rtm{all}{P} partly; \rtm{all}{D} were built then deferred by decision
(approval workflow), \rtm{all}{V} deviate by decision (open QR scans), \rtm{all}{N} are not started (mostly X12 EDI and
user administration) and \rtm{all}{O} targets are still to be agreed.
\end{keypoint}

\section{What it is not yet}
\begin{caution}[Proof of concept, not production]
\begin{itemize}
  \item One shared sign-in per portal (\code{admin} / \code{admin}); no personal accounts or role-based access.
  \item No review step before supplier data goes live (built earlier, archived).
  \item QR scans and their certificates are open to anyone holding the label.
  \item No X12 EDI or ERP integration; no production hosting, monitoring or security testing.
  \item OCR on real photos needs a tight crop and good light; accuracy not yet measured at scale.
\end{itemize}
\end{caution}

\section{Decisions needed}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Public QR scans}: keep open, or require a company sign-in before production?
  \item \textbf{Approval}: when should supplier data need company approval again, and who approves?
  \item \textbf{Address}: the permanent web address to print in QR codes.
  \item \textbf{Ownership}: owners and budget for Phase 1, and for risks R-01 (shared sign-in), R-02 (public scans) and
    R-04 (credentials shared during set-up must be rotated).
\end{enumerate}
