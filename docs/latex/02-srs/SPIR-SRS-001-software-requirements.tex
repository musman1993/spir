\section{Introduction}
\subsection{Purpose}
This Software Requirements Specification (SRS) states what SPIR must do. It describes the application as built in
release \SPIRRelease, including the decisions taken with the stakeholder during the project, and marks what is
deferred to later phases. It is written for the vendor and developers who will build on it, for testers, and for the
business owner who accepts it.

\subsection{Scope}
SPIR is a company-owned registry of \textbf{supplier product identity, manufacturing traceability and certificates}.
Suppliers enter the data for each shipment and attach a QR label to the parts. At receiving, anyone can scan the label
to see the shipment's data and certificates, and company staff can check the supplier's own label against the
registry with a photo.

\begin{keypoint}[In one sentence]
SPIR replaces paper and e-mail traceability with one registry per shipment, reachable from a QR label on the part.
\end{keypoint}

Business objectives: fewer receiving errors (wrong heat, wrong quantity, wrong part), faster inspection, certificates
available at the part, and an audit trail of every change.

\section{Overall description}
\subsection{Product perspective}
SPIR is a new, self-contained web application. It consists of a single-page web app, a REST API running as a Neon
Function, a Postgres database and a private object store. It does not yet exchange data with company ERP or supplier
EDI systems; X12 832/841 intake is a planned channel.

\Diagram[0.42]{context}{System context.}

\subsection{Product functions}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Two portals with their own sign-in: supplier and company.
  \item Supplier profile: the supplier the supplier account belongs to.
  \item Shipment data entry, one at a time (form) or many at once (CSV), with validation.
  \item Versioned history: every change is a new version; nothing is overwritten.
  \item Certificates (MTC, CoC, RT report and others) attached to shipments.
  \item One QR code per shipment; printing on A4 sticker sheets with the company logo.
  \item QR scanning by anyone with a phone, showing the live shipment data and certificates.
  \item Company search across all suppliers' shipments.
  \item Label check: photo of a supplier label, read by OCR and barcode decoding, matched and compared field by field.
  \item Audit trail of business-significant actions.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Operating environment}
Current desktop and mobile browsers (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox; last two major versions); camera access needs
HTTPS. API on Neon Functions (Node 24), Postgres 17 and S3-compatible object storage in Neon (us-east-2). Development
in GitHub Codespaces; hosting of the web app on GitHub Pages.

\subsection{Design and implementation constraints}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Stack chosen by the stakeholder: GitHub Codespaces, GitHub Actions, GitHub Pages, Neon Functions, Neon
    Postgres (JSONB for flexible attributes, no MongoDB), Neon Object Storage (private).
  \item No secrets in the web app; the browser never connects to the database.
  \item The repository is public: only synthetic data in committed files; real data in git-ignored folders.
  \item OCR must run in the browser for the proof of concept (no paid OCR service).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Assumptions and dependencies}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Suppliers will attach the SPIR QR label to parts before shipping.
  \item The company receiving label and the supplier label carry the identification fields listed in section~\ref{sec:data}.
  \item Neon and GitHub services are available; phone cameras can read standard QR codes.
\end{itemize}

\section{Functional requirements}

\subsection{Portals and sign-in (PT)}
\begin{reqs}
  SPIR-FR-PT-01 & SPIR shall provide a supplier portal at \code{/\#/supplier} and a company portal at \code{/\#/company}, each with its own sign-in page. & \must & \Done & Stakeholder\\
  SPIR-FR-PT-02 & In this phase each portal shall accept the username \code{admin} and password \code{admin}; any other combination shall be refused with the same message. & \must & \Done & Stakeholder\\
  SPIR-FR-PT-03 & A successful sign-in shall create a signed session valid for 12 hours; tampered or expired sessions shall be refused. & \must & \Done & \req{NFR-SEC-001}\\
  SPIR-FR-PT-04 & Each portal shall only reach its own functions: suppliers cannot run label checks or search other suppliers; the company cannot import or print labels. & \must & \Done & \req{FR-SUP-004}\\
  SPIR-FR-PT-05 & Sessions of the two portals shall be independent in one browser. & \must & \Done & Defect found in testing\\
  SPIR-FR-PT-06 & Individual user accounts with roles (RBAC), password policy and company single sign-on. & \must & \NotStarted & \req{FR-SUP-003}\\

\subsection{Supplier profile (SP)}
\begin{reqs}
  SPIR-FR-SP-01 & The supplier account shall belong to exactly one supplier, defined in its profile (name required; country, contact person, e-mail, phone, address optional). & \must & \Done & Stakeholder\\
  SPIR-FR-SP-02 & The supplier portal shall ask for the profile before shipments, CSV upload or QR labels can be used. & \must & \Done & Stakeholder\\
  SPIR-FR-SP-03 & Every shipment created by the supplier account shall carry the profile supplier; supplier columns in a CSV shall be ignored. & \must & \Done & Stakeholder\\
  SPIR-FR-SP-04 & Profile changes shall apply to existing shipments and be audited. & \should & \Done & \req{FR-SUP-008}\\
\end{reqs}

\subsection{Shipments (SH)}
\begin{reqs}
  SPIR-FR-SH-01 & A supplier shall be able to add one shipment with a form containing the identification fields of section~\ref{sec:data}. & \must & \Done & Stakeholder\\

  SPIR-FR-SH-03 & A supplier shall be able to upload many shipments as one CSV file, one row per shipment, with a downloadable template. & \must & \Done & Stakeholder\\
  SPIR-FR-SH-04 & A CSV with any invalid row shall be rejected completely, listing every problem with its row number. & \must & \Done & Stakeholder\\
  SPIR-FR-SH-05 & A shipment shall be identified by supplier, part number, PO number and PO line. Saving data for an existing shipment shall update it, not duplicate it. & \must & \Done & Stakeholder\\
  SPIR-FR-SH-06 & In an edit, part number, PO number and PO line shall be read-only. & \must & \Done & Stakeholder\\
  SPIR-FR-SH-07 & Saved data shall be live immediately (no approval step in this phase). & \must & \Done & Stakeholder\\
  SPIR-FR-SH-08 & Draft, submit, review, approve and reject with reasons, before data goes live. & \should & \Deferred & \req{FR-WF-001}--\req{FR-WF-009}\\
\end{reqs}

\subsection{Versions and history (VR)}

  SPIR-FR-VR-02 & Saving identical data shall not create a version. & \must & \Done & Stakeholder\\

\subsection{Certificates (DC)}
\begin{reqs}

  SPIR-FR-DC-02 & Files shall be checked for size, extension and real content type, stored privately with a SHA-256 hash, and downloadable only through links valid for 5 minutes. & \must & \Done & \req{FR-DOC-005}, \req{FR-DOC-006}\\
  SPIR-FR-DC-03 & Uploading a file shall never replace an earlier one. & \must & \Done & \req{FR-DOC-004}\\

\subsection{QR codes and labels (QR)}
\begin{reqs}
  SPIR-FR-QR-01 & Each shipment shall have exactly one active QR code, issued when it is first saved. & \must & \Done & \req{FR-QR-001}\\
  SPIR-FR-QR-02 & The QR code shall contain only a link with a random 16-character token; the token shall be stored hashed for lookup and encrypted for reprinting. & \must & \Done & \req{FR-QR-002}\\
  SPIR-FR-QR-03 & Printed labels shall keep working after any data change. & \must & \Done & Stakeholder\\
  SPIR-FR-QR-04 & Labels shall be readable by ordinary phone cameras: at least a 4-module white border, size-optimised error correction, vector output. & \must & \Done & Stakeholder\\
  SPIR-FR-QR-05 & The company logo shall sit in the centre of the code; every code shall be decoded again after drawing, and the logo shrunk or removed if decoding fails. & \must & \Done & Stakeholder\\
  SPIR-FR-QR-06 & Labels shall show Part \#, PO \# (with line) and Heat \# beside the code and print on A4 sticker sheets of 8, 14 or 21, with start position and printer alignment. & \must & \Done & Stakeholder\\
  SPIR-FR-QR-07 & A supplier shall replace (rotate) or revoke a label; the old code then answers ``revoked''. The shipment keeps its ID. & \must & \Done & \req{FR-QR-005}\\
\end{reqs}

\subsection{Public scan (PS)}

\subsection{Label check (LC)}
\begin{reqs}
  SPIR-FR-LC-01 & The company shall check a supplier label from a photo or camera capture, with a crop tool and automatic upscaling. & \must & \Done & \req{FR-OCR-001}, \req{FR-OCR-002}\\
  SPIR-FR-LC-02 & OCR and barcode decoding shall run in the browser; the server shall receive text, word confidences and barcodes. & \must & \Done & \req{FR-OCR-003}, \req{FR-OCR-007}\\
  SPIR-FR-LC-03 & Fields shall be extracted using a versioned label profile (aliases, patterns, fallbacks, thresholds). & \must & \Done & \req{FR-OCR-004}\\
  SPIR-FR-LC-04 & The shipment shall be resolved from identifier combinations (part + PO or heat; PO + heat or line); ambiguous cases go to the user. & \must & \Done & \req{FR-OCR-008}\\
  SPIR-FR-LC-05 & Each field shall get one result: MATCH, MISMATCH, LOW\_CONFIDENCE, UNREADABLE, MISSING\_ON\_LABEL, MISSING\_IN\_REGISTRY, NOT\_APPLICABLE, NOT\_CHECKED, REVIEW\_REQUIRED; the run one overall status: PASS, FAIL, PASS\_WITH\_WARNINGS, NEEDS\_REVIEW, UNRESOLVED. & \must & \Done & \req{FR-OCR-014}\\
  SPIR-FR-LC-06 & A confidence threshold shall never turn unequal values into a match; likely OCR confusions (8/B, 0/O, 1/I) go to review, never to a match. & \must & \Done & \req{FR-OCR-017}\\
  SPIR-FR-LC-07 & The operator shall confirm or correct fields without losing the raw OCR; corrections are attributed and audited. & \must & \Done & \req{FR-OCR-012}\\
  SPIR-FR-LC-08 & Label checks and discrepancies shall never change registry data. & \must & \Done & \req{FR-OCR-013}\\
  SPIR-FR-LC-09 & Each run shall pin the record version, label profile version and rule versions. & \must & \Done & \req{FR-OCR-016}\\
\end{reqs}

\subsection{Business rules}
{\small\zebra
\begin{xltabular}{\linewidth}{@{}L{1.4cm}Y@{}}
  \thead\hd{ID} & \hd{Rule}\\\endhead
  BR-01 & A shipment is identified by supplier + part number + PO number + PO line (the record key \code{PO-L<line>}).\\
  BR-02 & A product is identified by supplier + company part number.\\
  BR-03 & One active QR token per shipment; replacing it revokes the old one.\\
  BR-04 & A version is never edited; a change creates a new one (database trigger rejects edits of approved versions).\\
  BR-05 & Label checks never change registry data.\\
  BR-06 & A CSV import is all-or-nothing.\\
  BR-07 & Heat and batch are many-to-many; a version may list several heats.\\
  BR-08 & New CSV columns become new attribute definitions automatically; retired columns are rejected.\\
\end{xltabular}}

\section{External interface requirements}
\paragraph{User interface} Responsive web pages using the approved cozy-glass design (brand blue \code{\#004B8D}, cyan
\code{\#00A4D2}, status colours), usable from 360\,px wide; touch targets of at least 44\,px on the scan page.
\paragraph{Software interface} REST JSON API under \code{/api/v1}, documented in \fpath{api/openapi.yaml}; bearer-token
authentication; public endpoints \code{/qr/\{token\}} and \code{/qr/\{token\}/documents/\{code\}}.
\paragraph{Hardware} Phone and tablet cameras, webcams, HID keyboard-wedge barcode scanners (USB or Bluetooth), office
printers for A4 sticker sheets.
\paragraph{Communication} HTTPS only; database over TLS (\code{sslmode=verify-full}); object storage over the S3 API.
